# Photography credits

Every photograph in this demonstration comes from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)
and is used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Images
are referenced by photo id in `src/data/products.ts` (see the `photo()` helper)
and served through `next/image`.

## A note on what is *not* here

The catalogue was audited frame by frame before release. Photographs carrying a
visible third-party mark were removed rather than retouched, because NORTH 85 is
a fictional house and must not present another company's product as its own.
Frames whose art direction sat outside the charcoal / sand / taupe palette were
dropped for the same reason a real brand would drop them.
